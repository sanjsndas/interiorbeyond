    We write about the spaces that shape us.

    Studio Morphé is an independent editorial journal exploring interior design, architecture, and the built environment — from the smallest material detail to the largest urban gesture.

    Our Mission

    In a world of fleeting trends and disposable content, Studio Morphé exists to slow things down. We publish in-depth, carefully researched articles that examine not just what is being built, but why — and how it affects the way we live.

    We believe that good design is not a luxury reserved for the few. It is a fundamental part of how we experience daily life, and it deserves serious, accessible, and independent coverage. That's why we don't accept sponsored posts or native advertising. Our editorial decisions are ours alone.

    From practical guides on choosing materials to critical essays on the future of housing, our goal is to inform, provoke, and inspire — without ever talking down to our readers.

    What We Cover

    Our editorial scope is broad but focused. We cover the full spectrum of the built environment, with a particular emphasis on:

    • ◈

      Interior Design

      Residential and commercial interiors, color theory, spatial composition, and the art of making a room feel whole.

    • ◈

      Architecture

      Residential architecture, adaptive reuse, sustainable building practices, and the evolving relationship between structure and site.

    • ◈

      Materials & Craft

      Wood, stone, concrete, metal, and emerging bio-materials — how they perform, how they age, and how to specify them well.

    • ◈

      Space Planning

      Layout strategies, circulation, zoning, and the invisible logic that makes a home function effortlessly.

    • ◈

      Lighting & Atmosphere

      Natural and artificial light, layering, color temperature, and the sensory experience of a space.

    The Team

    Studio Morphé is edited by a small, dedicated team of writers and researchers. We work with a network of contributing architects, designers, and photographers to bring depth and perspective to every story.

    Elena Marchetti

    Editor-in-Chief

    Elena is an interiors writer and former practicing architect. She founded Studio Morphé in 2019 after a decade of writing for design magazines across Europe. She is based in Milan.

    Julian Hart

    Senior Editor

    Our Values

    Everything we publish is guided by a set of editorial principles that we take seriously:

    • 1

      Independence

      We do not accept sponsored content, paid placements, or affiliate-driven recommendations. Our only obligation is to our readers.

    • 2

      Depth over speed

      We would rather publish one thoroughly researched article than ten shallow ones. We take the time to verify facts, interview experts, and consider multiple perspectives.

    • 3

      Accessibility

      Design writing should not be exclusive. We explain complex ideas clearly, without jargon or condescension, so that both professionals and curious homeowners can benefit.

    • 4

      Sustainability

      We believe the built environment has a responsibility to the planet. We prioritize coverage of sustainable materials, passive design, and low-impact construction.
