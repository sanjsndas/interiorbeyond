    Cookies Policy

    How Studio Morphé uses cookies and similar technologies to improve your reading experience. We keep things transparent and minimal — just like our design philosophy.

    This Cookies Policy explains what cookies are, how we use them on studiomorphe.com (the "Site"), and your choices regarding their use. By continuing to browse the Site, you agree to our use of cookies as described in this policy.

    In short: We use a small number of essential cookies to make the Site work, plus optional analytics cookies to understand how readers use our articles. You can control or delete cookies at any time through your browser settings.

    1. What Are Cookies?

    Cookies are small text files that are stored on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently, provide a better user experience, and give site owners information about how their site is being used.

    Cookies can be "persistent" (remaining on your device until they expire or you delete them) or "session" cookies (deleted when you close your browser). They can also be "first-party" (set by us) or "third-party" (set by a service we use, such as an analytics provider).

    2. How We Use Cookies

    Studio Morphé is an independent editorial publication. We use cookies for a limited number of purposes, always with the goal of respecting your privacy while ensuring the Site functions properly.

    Specifically, we use cookies to:

    • Ensure the Site loads correctly and securely.
    • Remember your cookie consent preferences.
    • Understand which articles are most read, so we can improve our editorial coverage.
    • Measure site performance and diagnose technical issues.

    We do not use cookies for targeted advertising, and we do not sell your data to third parties.

    3. Types of Cookies We Use

    The table below lists the specific cookies that may be set when you visit the Site. We keep this list as current as possible, but third-party services may update their cookies from time to time.

    Cookie Name Type Purpose Duration
    cookie_consent Essential Stores your cookie preference so we don't ask again on every visit. 1 year
    session_id Essential Maintains basic session state for site functionality and security. Session
    _ga Analytics Used by Google Analytics to distinguish unique users and calculate article readership. 2 years
    _gid Analytics Used by Google Analytics to distinguish users for a 24-hour period. 24 hours
    _gat Analytics Used by Google Analytics to throttle request rates and improve performance. 1 minute

    Note: The analytics cookies listed above are only set if you consent to analytics tracking. You can withdraw consent at any time via the link in our footer or your browser settings.

    4. Third-Party Cookies

    Some cookies on the Site are set by third-party services that appear on our pages. We currently use the following third parties that may set cookies:

    • Google Analytics — to help us understand how readers navigate the Site and which content is most valuable. Google's privacy policy applies to data collected via these cookies.
    • Embedded content — if we embed images or media from platforms like Unsplash or YouTube, those platforms may set their own cookies. We do not control these cookies.

    We do not allow third parties to use cookies on our Site for advertising or re-targeting purposes.

    5. Your Cookie Choices

    You have several options for managing cookies:

    • Browser settings: Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. You can set your browser to refuse cookies, delete existing cookies, or alert you when a cookie is being set.
    • Opt-out tools: You can opt out of Google Analytics across all websites by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.
    • Consent withdrawal: If you previously consented to analytics cookies, you may withdraw your consent at any time by clearing cookies in your browser and adjusting your preferences on your next visit.

    Please note that disabling essential cookies may affect the functionality of the Site. If you block all cookies, some features may not work as intended.

    6. Do Not Track Signals

    Some browsers offer a "Do Not Track" (DNT) feature. Because there is not yet a common industry standard for how to interpret DNT signals, our Site does not currently respond to DNT requests. We do, however, limit our tracking to the essential and analytics purposes described above.

    7. Changes to This Policy

    We may update this Cookies Policy from time to time to reflect changes in technology, regulation, or our practices. When we make material changes, we will update the "Updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
